Overdue and risks

Overdue is measured, not opinion: ageing in QA, work in flight untouched, scopes behind a straight line to 5 Sep, and people whose in-flight work runs past it. Each risk carries the number it rests on, so it can be checked, and a mitigation someone can act on this week.

Signals

Scope health and slack

Click any number to open exactly that slice of the scope in the board below — delivered, in flight, requirements, flags. Progress is weighted across the items in build (tasks and bugs, plus stories delivered directly), with Dev Completed and everything after it counting in full. Slack = progress against a straight-line burn to 5 Sep, in days. Forecast clears the remaining backlog at the rate that stream has been running.

Progress weights: Dev Completed, QA, Resolved, Production Ready, Closed and Done count 1.0, Active, In Progress and Returned 0.3, New 0. Delivered means the code is written and merged — Dev Completed onwards. Everything from Dev Completed to Closed carries the same weight, so a move from QA to Closed does not raise the percentage; only work leaving New or Active does, and newly created tickets lower it.
